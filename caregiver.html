 For approved foster and kinship caregivers

Tell us what they want. You do the shopping.

Build a list for each child in your home. Donors fund the gifts one by one, and the money comes to you, so you buy the right size in the right week. If a child outgrows something or changes their mind, you spend it on what they actually need.

Takes about ten minutes. You can come back to it.

Sizes stay yours

Donors never see a size, a school, a photo, or a last name. You buy the fit, so nobody else needs it.

Change it any time

Swap a gift for something better up until the day you shop. The funds follow the child, not the line item.

Paid straight to you

Direct deposit in one to two days through Stripe. No bank? A Visa gift card in the mail instead.
