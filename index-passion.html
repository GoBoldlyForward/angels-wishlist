Questions people ask before they give

Short version: you are choosing a gift, and what actually moves is a donation to Atlanta Angels. The long version is below, including the parts that are less flattering to us.

The honest version

You are choosing a gift. What moves is a donation.

Every family on this site is verified by Atlanta Angels, and every line was added by a caregiver for a child in their home. When you check out, what you chose is recorded against that child's list and the funds go out to their household, so you are empowering that caregiver to shop the specific items and sizes the kids in their care actually need.

What actually happens after you check out

You check outYour card is charged by Atlanta Angels. You get an itemized receipt naming each gift and each child.
The list closesOn December 8 we total what each household raised, line by line, and mark the list funded.
The household is paidFunds land in their bank account in one to two days through Stripe, or on a Visa gift card if they cannot use a bank.
They shopThe caregiver shops for their kids in the sizes that fit by then, and sends receipts back to us.
You hear backA thank-you note from the household in January. No child photos, ever, and no last names.
